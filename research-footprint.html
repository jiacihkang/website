Research Locations

📌 Phase 1 — Research Exploration

At the beginning of my PhD, I explored multiple research questions in palaeontology and life sciences, focusing on proboscideans and fossil micro-CT analysis.

📌 Phase 2 — Method Development

During this phase, I developed workflows for imaging and analyzing micro-CT scans, optimized analytical pipelines, and collaborated with international labs and researchers.

📌 Phase 3 — Field Work & Data Collection

Collected samples, conducted field work, and integrated field data with laboratory imaging results. Documented workflows and evaluated results for publication.

📌 Phase 4 — Writing & Dissemination

Focused on drafting manuscripts, preparing conference presentations, and sharing findings with the academic community and collaborators.
